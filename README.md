One fortune cookie line for a crypto trader who just got liquidated — written
so the trader laughs instead of cries.

> The stars advise you to sell everything — they are three hours too late.

The joke works because the fortune-teller is not a person — it is the stars
themselves, and they are useless in exactly the way a fortune cookie should
be: they solemnly advise the trader to sell everything, three hours after
the liquidation already sold it all for him. The setup sounds like real
oracular wisdom ("The stars advise you…"), so the punchline lands as a
betrayal of that authority — the universe finally speaks, and its advice is
perfect except for being late. No margin puns, no "rekt", no second sentence
needed: one voice, one read, one very late universe.

Why "three hours" earns its place: a vague "too late" would be a shrug, but
a specific number makes the lateness concrete — the stars checked their
charts, did the math, and still missed the liquidation by an afternoon.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22+, stdlib only, deterministic) writes the single fortune
line to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (falls back to `/output`).

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=/tmp/out node main.ts
cat /tmp/out/entry.txt
```

## Entry

- **Text:** "The stars advise you to sell everything — they are three hours too late."
- **Length:** 72 characters, 14 words — inside the gate (1–160 characters, max 20 words).
